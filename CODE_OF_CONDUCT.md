# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in Einsteinminds a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender
identity, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

Because Einsteinminds serves children and learners worldwide, we hold
ourselves to an especially high standard of care and respect.

## Our Standards

**Positive behavior includes:**
- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for learners

**Unacceptable behavior includes:**
- Harassment, trolling, or personal attacks
- Publishing others' private information
- Any conduct that could endanger or harm children
- Introducing surveillance, manipulation, or exploitative mechanics

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project team at
**conduct@einsteinminds.org**. All complaints will be reviewed and
investigated, resulting in a response deemed necessary and appropriate.
